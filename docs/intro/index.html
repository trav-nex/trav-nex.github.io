Welcome to WIIL

WIIL is not a chatbot. It's an AI-powered business growth and operations platform. WIIL helps businesses get discovered, present their offerings, and turn customer intent into completed transactions — through SEO-ready web catalogs and catalog-aware AI agents across web, phone, chat, SMS, and email. No code required.

Get Discovered

Instant, SEO-ready web catalogs that help customers find your menus, products, services, and listings online.

Catalog-Aware Agents

AI grounded in your real business data — pricing, availability, inventory, and policies — not generic FAQs.

Complete Transactions

Customers book, order, reserve, apply, and qualify inside the conversation — 24/7, not callbacks.

No-Code or API

Launch from the dashboard, or build on the REST API and official SDKs — your choice.

From discovery to completed transaction​

Whether a customer starts on a catalog page, a phone call, a chat, an SMS, or an email, WIIL uses the same managed business data to answer, route, guide, and complete the next step.

Catalog Management → Discovery → AI-Guided Interaction → Transaction → Revenue

This is the core idea behind every WIIL capability: customers discover your business, explore real offerings, ask questions through the right agent for the channel, and complete the next business step — while you manage catalog content once and publish it everywhere it matters.

The problem WIIL solves​

Every business faces the same challenge: customers expect instant, around-the-clock service, but staffing 24/7 is expensive and impractical. Customers can't find accurate menus, products, or listings online. Website visitors leave with unanswered questions. Missed calls and slow responses become lost revenue.

Most customers who reach voicemail or wait for a callback never follow up. Every missed call, abandoned visit, and after-hours inquiry is potential revenue lost.

WIIL closes that gap with catalog-aware AI agents that:

  • Make your offerings discoverable, searchable, and conversational
  • Answer every call and message instantly, in context
  • Handle bookings, orders, reservations, applications, and lead qualification
  • Operate 24/7 without 24/7 staffing
  • Escalate complex issues to a human when needed

Reach customers proactively​

Beyond inbound conversations, WIIL gives developers a unified outbound communications layer — send email, SMS, and AI voice-agent calls from one SDK, with centralized templates and production infrastructure already built in. One pattern, three channels.

Email

Transactional and marketing email with HTML + text bodies, CC/BCC, and reply-to.

SMS

Reminders, confirmations, verification codes, and tracking links over text.

AI Voice Calls

Outbound calls handled by a configured AI agent — immediate or scheduled.

One SDK, one pattern, three channels:

// Email
await client.outboundEmails.create({ to, templateId, templateVariables });

// SMS
await client.outboundSms.create({ to, from, templateId, templateVariables });

// AI voice call
await client.outboundCalls.create({ to, from, agentConfigurationId, scheduleType: 'scheduled', scheduledAt });

Centralized templates live in the platform — so marketing can update copy with no code deploy, brand stays consistent, and required variables are validated at send time. The platform also handles the production layer for you:

ConcernPlatform handles
DeliveryProvider failover, retry with exponential backoff
QueuingHigh-volume batching, rate limiting, priority
SchedulingFuture sends and timezone-aware delivery windows
TrackingDelivery status, bounce handling, engagement metrics
ComplianceAudit logs, unsubscribe management, data retention

Use it for transactional notifications, appointment and booking reminders, verification codes, reservation confirmations, and AI-powered follow-up calls.

Manage many locations from one platform​

Chains, franchises, clinics, restaurant groups, property portfolios, and multi-branch teams keep shared brand control while each location uses its own hours, inventory, pricing, services, policies, and availability.

  • Global catalog control, local availability — shared templates with location-specific overrides
  • Smart routing — send customers to the right location automatically
  • Location-aware agents — per business, region, or location
  • Per-location reporting — compare performance across sites

Built-in CRM​

One of WIIL's most powerful features is its automated CRM. Unlike traditional systems where staff manually enter customer data, the AI agent:

  • Auto-qualifies leads — naturally requests name, phone, and email during conversations
  • Recognizes customers — identifies returning customers by name or phone number
  • Tracks preferences — records communication preferences for automated follow-ups
  • Sends confirmations — automatically emails or texts booking confirmations

Getting started​

Ready to deploy your first AI agent? Here's the path:

  1. Create your account — sign up with email, Google, or GitHub
  2. Complete business setup — select your industry and configure your business profile
  3. Build your catalog & agent — choose voice, language, and add your knowledge base
  4. Test and deploy — preview your agent, then go live with one click

What you'll need​

  • A WIIL account — sign up for free
  • A basic understanding of your business requirements:
    • What tasks should your agent handle?
    • Which channels will customers use to reach you?
    • What knowledge should your agent have access to?

Start building​

Open the User Guide to begin:

  • Understanding Assistants — learn about agent configuration and capabilities
  • Platform Services — configure appointments, reservations, and more
