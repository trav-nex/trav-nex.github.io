Platform Features

WIIL is the AI infrastructure that takes a customer from discovery to completed transaction — across web, phone, chat, SMS, and email. Publish your catalog once, and a catalog-aware agent answers questions, books, orders, reserves, and qualifies leads on every channel. Launch from the dashboard with no code, or build on the REST API and SDKs.

The platform at a glance​

Conversational AI

Agents grounded in your real catalog — pricing, availability, and policies — not generic FAQs.

Voice & calling

Human-sounding STT to agent to TTS pipelines, with smart transfers to a person when needed.

Language & translation

Serve customers in 30+ languages with real-time, bidirectional voice translation.

Web catalogs

SEO-ready catalogs for menus, products, services, reservations, and property — each with an agent built in.

Transactions & operations

Customers book, order, reserve, and qualify inside the conversation — 24/7, not callbacks.

Outbound communications

One SDK for email, SMS, and AI voice calls — templates, scheduling, and delivery handled for you.

Analytics & insights

Conversation analytics, sentiment, and AI summaries with key points and action items.

Security & compliance

Multi-tenant isolation, project segmentation, token authentication, and configurable logging.

Voice & calling​

A professional phone presence without a call center — natural voice in, natural voice out, with the option to hand off to a human.

FeatureWhat it does
Speech-to-Text (STT)Convert voice to text with models like Deepgram, OpenAI Whisper, and Cartesia
Text-to-Speech (TTS)Generate natural voices from ElevenLabs, Cartesia, OpenAI, and more
Speech-to-Speech (STS)Direct voice-to-voice AI with Gemini and OpenAI for ultra-low latency
Provisioning chainsConfigure end-to-end voice pipelines (STT to agent to TTS)
Call transferBlind and warm transfers to route calls to a person when needed

Speak every customer's language. Real-time translation lets participants each hear their own language, naturally.

FeatureWhat it does

Bidirectional supportTranslate conversations in both directions for natural multilingual flow
Multi-participant sessionsMultiple participants, each hearing their native language
Transcription loggingCapture translations with timestamps and metadata for compliance and review
